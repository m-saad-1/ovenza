About This Admin Panel

This is a full-featured Restaurant Management Dashboard — a centralized command center built for modern food businesses. It streamlines every aspect of your operations, from taking orders at the counter to tracking deliveries and managing your team.

Dashboard Capabilities
Order Management
Track dine-in, takeaway, and delivery orders in real-time across all branches.
Point of Sale (POS)
A fast, touch-friendly POS terminal for walk-in orders with full cart management.
Kitchen Display (KDS)
Live kitchen board showing order tickets by status — New, Preparing, Ready.
Inventory & Supply
Track ingredient stock levels, suppliers, recipe costs, and waste logs.
Content & Media
Manage website banners, gallery, contact info, and opening hours from one place.
Analytics & Reports
Revenue charts, sales by channel, best-sellers, and period-over-period comparisons.
CRM & Loyalty
Customer profiles, order history, loyalty points, and review management.
Delivery Logistics
Assign riders, track deliveries live, and manage reservations and floor plans.
Finance & P&L
Revenue tracking, expenses, payroll, and profit & loss summaries per branch.
Built for Your Restaurant

Every restaurant is different. This panel is fully customizable — your business receives only the modules and features that are relevant to how you operate. Unnecessary sections can be hidden, renamed, or replaced with restaurant-specific workflows.

From branding and color themes to user roles and permissions, every element of this dashboard can be tailored. Whether you run a single outlet or a multi-branch chain, the panel scales to fit your exact needs.
