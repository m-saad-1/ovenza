# Complete Restaurant Management Platform Demo — Final Build & Sales Experience Prompt

## 1. Project Objective

Build a **complete, polished, responsive Restaurant Management & Ordering Platform demo** that is designed not only to demonstrate software functionality, but also to **sell the overall solution to restaurant owners**.

This is a **sales demo**, not a generic admin dashboard.

The client should be able to open the demo, explore it independently, understand what the system does, understand how it works for a restaurant, understand that the displayed restaurant is only an example, and clearly understand the commercial offer.

The final experience must communicate:

> **We provide the restaurant website and complete restaurant digital management system at no setup/development cost. You only pay a small monthly amount for the managed platform and services.**

The demo must feel like a genuine commercial SaaS/RMS platform rather than a portfolio template.

---

# 5. About Demo Interaction

The **About Demo button must behave exactly like the Demo Dashboard entry** in terms of interaction quality.

When clicked:

> Open a polished modal/dialog.

The modal must:

* Open smoothly
* Be centered on desktop
* Be responsive on mobile
* Have a proper backdrop
* Prevent accidental background interaction
* Support close button
* Close with Escape
* Have proper focus handling
* Scroll internally if necessary
* Never overflow the viewport

The modal should feel like a **product introduction/sales panel**, not a simple alert box.

---

# 6. About Demo Modal — Purpose

The modal must explain:

1. What this demo is.
2. What the restaurant website represents.
3. What the platform can do.
4. That the shown restaurant is only a demo/example.
5. That the actual website and dashboard will be customized.
6. That there are **no setup/development fees** under the advertised managed offer.
7. That the restaurant only pays a small monthly amount.
8. That the monthly service includes the complete platform and management.
9. That the client can view MhStudio services and pricing.

The modal should be visually strong enough that a restaurant owner immediately understands the offer.

# 8. About Demo — Main Explanation

Use clear, client-friendly language.

The main explanation should communicate:

> **This is a demonstration of a complete restaurant website and management platform. The restaurant, menu, branding, products, orders and data shown here are examples only.**

Then explain:

> **Your actual website and dashboard will be fully customized according to your restaurant, branding, menu, branches, order flow and business requirements.**

Avoid technical language such as React, API architecture, database schema, etc.

This modal is for restaurant owners, not developers.

---

# 9. Large Commercial Offer Section

This section should be the visual focal point of the modal.

Use a large highlighted area.

Heading:

> **NO SETUP OR DEVELOPMENT FEE**

Make this heading visually prominent.

Supporting text:

> **We provide your restaurant website and complete digital restaurant platform at no setup or development cost under our managed service model.**

Then a very large pricing statement:

> **Only PKR 5,000–10,000 / month**

Use a strong supporting explanation:

> **You simply pay a small monthly amount based on your restaurant's requirements and selected services.**

Important:

Do not visually imply that every restaurant automatically pays exactly PKR 5,000 or exactly PKR 10,000.

Communicate it as:

> **Typical monthly plans: PKR 5,000–10,000**

or:

> **Starting around PKR 5,000/month, depending on requirements.**

The exact package should be determined according to the restaurant's needs.
